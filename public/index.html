An exhilarating evening where bids rose with excitement and every paddle raised supported our student scholarship fund. Explore highlights, meet the team, and send us your questions below.

Ask a question

What is an English Auction?

Our event followed the classic English Auction format: the auctioneer announces a starting price and participants openly place progressively higher bids. The highest bid at the fall of the gavel wins.

Purpose of the auction

Proceeds support campus scholarships, innovation grants, and community outreach. Beyond fundraising, the evening celebrates craftsmanship and generosity from students, alumni, and local partners.

Who was in the room?

  • Auctioneer: The energetic host guiding the pace and confirming winning bids.
  • Bidders: Students, alumni, and patrons—paddles up, strategies out in the open.
  • Participants: Artists, makers, and clubs who consigned unique items and experiences.
